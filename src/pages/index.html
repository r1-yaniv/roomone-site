How it works

From partner to venture.

Every engagement follows the same path. We partner with a single firm, immerse in the business, and identify where technology moves the needle — then build it ourselves, MVP first. From there the work either stays embedded and compounds inside the portfolio, or spins off as its own venture. Either way, we're paid in equity.
