FAQ

The four questions we get asked in every first meeting.

Q.01

How are you different from a VC?

A VC picks companies and writes checks from a fund. We don't manage a fund and we don't write checks — we do the work. We embed inside your portfolio, build the technology ourselves, and earn equity in what we build. Think operating partner with an engineering arm, not investor with a term sheet.

Q.02

How are you different from an accelerator?

Accelerators run cohorts of external startups through a fixed program and take a slice of each. We have no cohort, no curriculum and no demo day. We are the founding team: we partner with one firm at a time, find the opportunity inside its portfolio, and build the venture ourselves — from MVP to hiring to scale.

Q.03

How can you understand our businesses if you don't come from that world?

Learning markets fast is the core of what we do. We sit with your operators, read the numbers, map the workflows, and force understanding through friction — then we prove it by shipping something real. What we bring is the rare combination: enough business depth to find what matters, and the technical ability to build it.

Q.04

How does the equity actually work?

Two ways. Solutions we build for portfolio companies earn us equity in the outcome they create. Ventures we spin off are mutually owned with our partner from day one. Either way, we're paid in ownership, not fees — we only make money when the things we build create value.
