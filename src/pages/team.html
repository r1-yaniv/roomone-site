Our story

It started in Room #1.

Before RoomOne was a firm, it was an actual room. Three of us, one apartment, and an instant click — the kind where the whiteboard fills up before the kettle boils. Business, product, engineering: different edges, same obsession with how things work and how they could work better.

It was only ever a matter of time until we built something together. Eventually we stopped waiting, shook hands, and partnered up. The name is a reminder of where every venture really starts: with the right people in the room.
